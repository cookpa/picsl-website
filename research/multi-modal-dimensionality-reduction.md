Our paper on multi-modal dimensionality reduction was recently accepted by *Methods*.  It shows how to use our sparse dimensionality reduction techniques to form interpretable predictive models from neuroimaging data.  All data and code are open-source.  Paper is available [here](/images/wp/2014/10/MultiModalEanat.pdf).
