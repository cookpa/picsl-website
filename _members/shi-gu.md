**Research Overview:**
I am a graduate student in the program of [Applied Mathematics and Computational Science](http://www.amcs.upenn.edu/) at University of Pennsylvania, conducting research under the supervision of [Prof. Jim Gee](/members/jim-gee.html) and [Prof. Danielle S. Bassett](http://www.danisbassett.com/group-members.html).  My work focuses on the application of network methods and statistical methods to neural imaging data. More detailedly, my researches cover three topics: the analysis of dynamics, the simulation of neural systems, and the statistical inference on the network type data.
**Education:**

- PhD in Applied Mathematics, University of Pennsylvania, 2016(expected)
- B.Sc in Mathematics and Physics, Tsinghua University, 2007-2011

**Projects:**

- [Controllability on the brain networks: compare three different control strategies on the brain networks generated from DSI data.](/research/controllability-on-brain-networks.html)
- Detection of structural state transition along neural development.
