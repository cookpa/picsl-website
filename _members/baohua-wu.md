**Research Overview:**
I am a Ph.D. student of [Computer and Information Science](http://www.cis.upenn.edu) working on medical image analysis under the supervision of [Prof. Jim Gee](/members/jim-gee.html). I received master and bachelor degrees in computer science from Tsinghua University. My research interests include optimization methods and parallel algorithms for image registration.
**Projects:**

- ITKv4: Quasi-Newton optimizer, parameter scale estimator and [GPU-based image registration](http://www.itk.org/Wiki/ITK/Release_4/GPU_Acceleration)
- GPU memory management for Demons registration
- [Interactive medical image registration](/research/interactive-image-registration.html) with bounded biharmonic functions
