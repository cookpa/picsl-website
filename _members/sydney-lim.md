Sydney Lim was a member of the PICSL research staff in the Department of Radiology, working on postmortem imaging studies of neurodegenerative disease. Sydney contributed to studies linking ex vivo MRI to pathology, including [tau pathology and cortical thinning in whole-hemisphere 7T MRI](/research/ptau-7t-morphometry.html) (2023), the [3D atlas of tau tangles in the medial temporal lobe](/research/mtl-tau-atlas.html) (2024), [MRI-guided histology for brain banking](/research/mri-guided-histology.html) (2025) and [subcortical atrophy and pathology](/research/postmortem-mri-subcortical-pathology.html) (2026), as well as [medial temporal lobe atrophy in semantic variant PPA](/research/svppa-mtl-atrophy.html) (2021) and an anatomically valid segmentation protocol for anterior medial temporal lobe regions (*Hippocampus*, 2025).
