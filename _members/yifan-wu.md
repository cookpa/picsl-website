Yifan Wu earned a Ph.D. in Bioengineering at Penn, advised by [Jim Gee](/members/jim-gee.html), with the dissertation "Knowledge-Integrated Learning for AI in Medical Image Analysis" (2024). Yifan's research spanned image registration and knowledge-guided medical AI: first author of [NODEO](/research/nodeo.html), a neural ODE framework for deformable image registration (CVPR 2022), and of a concept-based interpretable model for diagnosing choroid neoplasias from multimodal data (*Nature Communications*, 2025), and a contributor to "A Textbook Remedy for Domain Shifts: Knowledge Priors for Medical Image Analysis" (NeurIPS 2024).
