**Research Overview**
I’m a Ph.D. student in Applied Mathematics & Computational Science. I’m affiliated with [Complex Systems Group](http://www.danisbassett.com/) and [PiCSL (Penn Image Computing and Science Lab)](http://picsl.upenn.edu/). My research interests lie in the intersection of Network, Machine Learning and Statistics. In particular, my research focuses on finding statistical significant communities in networks, network diagnostics, and network classification. A major goal of our current work is to improve the understanding of brain network using advanced machine learning and statistics techniques.
