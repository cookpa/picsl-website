Abdullah Aly was a research assistant at PICSL, working with [Alison Pouch](/members/alison-pouch.html) and [Paul Yushkevich](/members/paul-yushkevich.html) on cardiac and medical image analysis. Abdullah contributed to [segmentation of the left ventricular mitral valve complex](/research/lv-mv-segmentation-stacom.html) (STACOM 2019), [fully automated modeling of the left ventricle and mitral valve](/research/lv-mv-modeling.html) (*Medical Image Analysis*, 2022), and an [open-source tool for designing synthetic skeletons](/research/synthetic-skeletons.html) for medial modeling (*Journal of Medical Imaging*, 2024).
