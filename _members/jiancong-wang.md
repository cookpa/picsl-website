Jiancong Wang was a doctoral student at PICSL whose work covered deep learning and image analysis across many applications. Jiancong contributed to [choroid segmentation in retinal OCT](/research/choroid-oct.html) (2017), the [ex vivo hippocampus atlas](/research/hippocampus-atlas-pnas.html) (*PNAS*, 2018), [diffeomorphic medial modeling](/research/diffeomorphic-medial-modeling.html) (IPMI 2019), AI systems for differential diagnosis on brain MRI ([Radiology](/research/ai-ddx-brain-mri.html) and [Radiology: Artificial Intelligence](/research/deep-gray-ddx.html), 2020), placenta segmentation from 3D ultrasound (2020, 2022), DeepAtrophy (2021), and deep label fusion for medical image segmentation (*Medical Image Analysis*, 2023).
