This was my brain on grad school

I am currently a Research Specialist at Penn working in the  [Penn Image Computing & Science Laboratory](http://picsl.upenn.edu).  The primary focus of my work is the development of methods for using multivariate neuroimaging data to examine structural and functional networks in the brain in order to learn how their properties relate to behavior.
More info: [Google Scholar](http://scholar.google.com/citations?user=6lyYDNEAAAAJ "Google Scholar") / [Research Gate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Jeffrey_Duda) / [Homepage](http://www.jeffreyduda.com)
My work has led to involvement in a number of research projects & topics such as:

- [Traumatic brain injury](/research/brain-injury.html "Brain Injury")
- [Measuring brain function and connectivity with MRI](/research/brain-function.html "Brain Function")
