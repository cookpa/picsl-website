Sadhana Ravikumar earned a Ph.D. in Bioengineering at Penn, advised by [Paul Yushkevich](/members/paul-yushkevich.html), with the dissertation "Characterizing medial temporal lobe neurodegeneration due to tau pathology in Alzheimer's disease using postmortem imaging" (2022), and continued at PICSL as a postdoctoral researcher. Sadhana's work combined ex vivo MRI with serial histology to map where tau tangles accumulate in the medial temporal lobe and how they relate to neurodegeneration, including an [ex vivo atlas of the earliest regions affected by Alzheimer's disease](/research/ex-vivo-mtl-atlas-isbi.html) (ISBI 2020), an ex vivo MRI atlas of the MTL (*Acta Neuropathologica Communications*, 2021), and a [3D atlas of tau tangles in the medial temporal lobe](/research/mtl-tau-atlas.html) (*Nature Communications*, 2024). Sadhana received Penn's 2023 Solomon R. Pollack Award for Excellence in Graduate Bioengineering Research.

Sadhana is now a Senior R&D Engineer on the Medical Computing team at [Kitware](https://www.kitware.com/sadhana-ravikumar/).
