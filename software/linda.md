# LINDA

![](/images/wp/2017/07/LINDA-300x150.png)
An R toolkit for automatic segmentation of chronic stroke lesions. [LINDA](https://dorianps.github.io/LINDA/) uses hierarchical predictions from low to high resolution, to achieve an iterative improvement of the segmentation. Relies on ANTsR for image manipulation and uses pre-trained random forest models for the prediction of the lesion. Details are available in [Pustina et al. (Hum Brain Mapp. 2016 Apr;37(4):1405-21)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/26756101).
